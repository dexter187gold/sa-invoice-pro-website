Privacy notice

Website and checkout information · Last updated 27 October 2026

SA Invoice Pro aims to collect only the information needed to answer enquiries, process purchases and associate a licence with the right account. This notice describes the current website and checkout flow; it is not a substitute for a complete legal review of all product services.

Information we may receive

  • Contact details you provide, such as your name and email address.
  • Plan and purchase references required to process checkout and licence activation.
  • Technical request information collected by the website host for security, reliability and troubleshooting.

Payments

Payment details are entered on PayFast's payment pages. SA Invoice Pro's website should not ask you to send card details, banking passwords or one-time PINs by email. PayFast and the billing service process payment and transaction data under their own terms and privacy practices.

How information is used

Information may be used to respond to support requests, associate a licence with your account email, reconcile a purchase, prevent fraud and maintain the service. Do not send sensitive identity or payment credentials to support.

Sharing and service providers

The website is hosted through Cloudflare, and checkout/licensing is handled by the SA Invoice Pro billing service and PayFast when configured. Relevant information may be processed by these providers to operate the requested service.

Retention and requests

Purchase and licence records may be retained for operational, accounting, fraud-prevention and legal purposes. For questions or a request relating to your personal information, contact sainvoicepro@gmail.com. Please use the email associated with your account and do not include passwords or payment credentials.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the website and checkout flow evolve. The current version will be published on this page.
