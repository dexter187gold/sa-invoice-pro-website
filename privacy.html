Privacy policy.

This page describes the general approach for information submitted through the marketing website, checkout and support channels.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

SA Invoice Pro aims to handle personal information responsibly. This page is general information and should be reviewed against the actual data flows and applicable South African law before being treated as a final legal notice.

Information you provide

If you contact support or begin a licence purchase, you may provide your name, email address, plan selection and information about your enquiry or payment reference.

Payments

Checkout is intended to be processed by PayFast through the SA Invoice Pro billing service. Do not submit card or banking credentials to this website by email. Payment information is handled according to the relevant payment provider's process.

How information is used

  • To respond to support and product enquiries.
  • To prepare and administer licence purchases.
  • To investigate payment or activation issues.
  • To maintain and secure the website and its services.

Sharing and retention

Information may be processed by service providers that support hosting, licensing, communications and payment processing, only as needed for those functions. Retention periods and access controls should be reviewed as the service evolves.

Your choices

For questions about information associated with your enquiry or licence, contact sainvoicepro@gmail.com.
