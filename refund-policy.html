Refund & cancellation policy.

This policy explains how to contact SA Invoice Pro about a software licence cancellation, payment query or refund request.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Business scope: This policy relates only to SA Invoice Pro, a software development product for South African service teams. The Paystack business account identified for this product is used for software development / SA Invoice Pro payments. Electronic repair, hardware sales and other electronics services are not covered by this account or policy.

1. Software and licence purchases

SA Invoice Pro is business desk software. The application displays the available trial and licence options, their features, term, seat limits and applicable price. Please review the current details shown in the application before confirming a purchase. The checkout amount and terms presented by the billing provider at the time of payment apply to that transaction.

The website's Plans & Pricing page is a guide to the available options. If any website information differs from the current in-app checkout, rely on the current in-app details and contact support if anything is unclear.

2. Payment and activation

Customers can follow the payment options presented on the SA Invoice Pro licence page. Where Paystack is selected, payment is processed through Paystack. A return to the app or website after checkout is not, by itself, confirmation of payment. The transaction must be verified by the billing service before the related licence is activated or an activation key is issued.

Keep the payment reference and the email address used for the purchase. Never send full card details, passwords, account PINs or one-time passcodes to support.

3. Cancellation requests

If you wish to cancel a purchase, stop a renewal where a renewal option has been presented, or ask for a paid licence to be cancelled, contact us using the details in section 5. Include your account email and payment reference. We will review the request against the purchase details and any terms presented at checkout.

Closing the app, uninstalling it or simply stopping use does not notify us of a cancellation request. Please contact support so that the request can be identified and reviewed.

4. Refund requests

Refund requests are reviewed individually, taking account of the transaction, the service supplied, the information presented at purchase and applicable South African law. This policy does not remove or limit any consumer right or remedy that applies by law.

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, charged more than once, paid but did not receive the licence, or experienced a material problem with the purchased service, contact us promptly. We will investigate and explain the available next steps. Any refund or other remedy will be handled in accordance with applicable law and the circumstances of the transaction.

A refund is not automatically guaranteed simply because a customer changes their mind or does not use the software. This statement does not exclude any statutory cooling-off, cancellation or refund right that may apply to your purchase.

5. How to contact us

Email sainvoicepro@gmail.com with the subject SA Invoice Pro — Cancellation or Refund Request. Please include:

  • Your name and the email address used for the SA Invoice Pro account;
  • Your payment / Paystack reference, if available;
  • The plan or licence purchased, payment date and amount;
  • A brief explanation of the issue and the outcome you are requesting.

We may ask for reasonable information to verify the transaction. Do not include sensitive payment credentials in your email. We will respond using the contact details you provide.

6. Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to contract out of any right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable South African consumer-protection or electronic-transaction laws. If you are unsure how a legal right applies to your transaction, you may seek independent advice.
