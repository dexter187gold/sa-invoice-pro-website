Refund & cancellation.

How refunds, cancellations and payment issues are handled for SA Invoice Pro software licences.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy applies to purchases of SA Invoice Pro software licences and related product payments processed for the software business (software development). It does not cover unrelated hardware or electronic-repair services that may be operated under a separate merchant profile.

What you are buying

SA Invoice Pro licences are sold as single-purchase software licences unless a separate written agreement states otherwise. Plan details and seat limits are described on the pricing page.

Cancellation

  • There is no recurring subscription to cancel for the standard single-purchase licence plans shown on this website.
  • If a future recurring plan is offered, you may cancel renewal at any time before the next billing date by contacting support. Cancellation stops future charges; it does not automatically refund amounts already paid for the current period.

Refunds

  • Duplicate or erroneous charges: Contact us within 14 days of the charge with your payment reference. Valid duplicate charges are refunded in full.
  • Service not provided: If a paid licence cannot be activated or used because of a fault on our side, we will either restore access or refund the affected payment after verification.
  • Change of mind: Once a licence has been issued and activated for the intended account, fees are generally non-refundable, except where South African consumer law requires otherwise or we agree a refund in writing.
  • Partial use: Refunds are not ordinarily given solely because a feature set or seat count no longer matches your preference after activation.

How to request a refund

Email sainvoicepro@gmail.com with:

  • The email address used at checkout
  • Payment or order reference
  • Date and amount of the charge
  • A short description of the issue

We aim to respond within 5 business days. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method where the payment provider allows it.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before raising a chargeback so we can resolve the matter directly. Chargebacks opened without first contacting support may delay investigation of a genuine refund request.
