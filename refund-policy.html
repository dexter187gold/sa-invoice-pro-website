Payments, annual licences & support

Refund & cancellation policy.

This policy applies to SA Invoice Pro software licences sold for the SA Invoice Pro business desk application.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Business scope: SA Invoice Pro is a software development product for South African service teams. This merchant account and policy cover the SA Invoice Pro software only. They do not cover electronic repair, hardware sales, device repairs or other electronics services.

1. What the licence includes

The app currently presents a 7-day free trial and the following annual software licence options. Prices below are the app's published guide prices in South African rand (ZAR); the final amount displayed by the billing service at checkout takes precedence if a price has been updated.

  • Trial: Free for 7 days, with limited access to clients, invoices and quotes.
  • Solo: R1,999 per year; 1 device seat.
  • Shop: R4,999 per year; up to 5 seats.
  • Multi-branch: R11,999 per year; up to 25 seats and multi-branch workspace features.

Lifetime and Enterprise access are not standard self-service checkout plans. They are issued by an authorised administrator and may be subject to separate written commercial terms.

2. Payment and activation

Online licence payments are directed through the SA Invoice Pro app's Paystack checkout. Payment methods available to a customer are the methods displayed by Paystack at the time of checkout and may depend on eligibility and availability. A payment is not treated as successful merely because a customer returns to the app or website: the payment must be verified by the billing service before a licence or activation key is issued.

Keep your payment reference and the email address used at checkout. Do not send card details, passwords, PINs or one-time passcodes to support.

3. Trial, cancellation and renewal

  • You may leave the free trial without making a purchase. No paid licence is created by simply using the trial.
  • The paid Solo, Shop and Multi-branch options are annual licences. They are not monthly plans.
  • If you decide not to continue, do not make a renewal purchase when the current annual term ends. If an automatic renewal is expressly offered in a future checkout, its frequency, amount and cancellation method will be disclosed before you authorise it.
  • If you have already paid and need the licence cancelled, contact support with your account email and payment reference. Cancellation of access does not by itself determine whether a refund is due.
  • If you abandon or close a checkout before payment is completed, no licence should be activated from that incomplete transaction. If your bank or payment provider nevertheless shows a debit, contact us with the transaction reference so it can be investigated.

4. Refunds

Refund requests are reviewed individually and in accordance with applicable South African consumer-protection and electronic-transaction laws. Nothing in this policy removes or limits a right that cannot lawfully be excluded.

  • Duplicate or incorrect payment: We will investigate verified duplicate charges, incorrect amounts and payments applied to the wrong order. Where the error is confirmed, the excess or incorrect payment will be refunded or corrected.
  • Licence not delivered: If payment is verified but we cannot provide the purchased licence because of a fault within our control, we will work to restore delivery. If we cannot resolve the issue within a reasonable time, we will assess an appropriate refund.
  • Material service failure: If a material fault prevents the paid service from being supplied as described, contact support. We will investigate and provide the remedy required by law or otherwise agreed in writing.
  • Change of mind: A change-of-mind request after activation is not automatically refundable. However, this does not affect any applicable statutory cooling-off, cancellation or refund rights.
  • Unused time or features: A request based only on not using the service or not using every included feature is not, by itself, a guarantee of a pro-rata refund. Applicable statutory rights remain unaffected.

Where a statutory cooling-off right applies to the transaction, it will be honoured subject to the relevant legal conditions and exceptions. The treatment of digital services or content already supplied may depend on the consent and disclosures given at purchase.

5. How to request a refund or cancellation

Email sainvoicepro@gmail.com and include:

  • The name and email address used for the SA Invoice Pro account;
  • Your Paystack transaction/payment reference;
  • The plan purchased, payment date and amount;
  • A short explanation of the issue and the outcome you are requesting.

We aim to acknowledge and respond to requests within 5 business days. We may request reasonable information needed to verify the transaction. Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method where the payment provider supports this; the provider or bank may require additional processing time.

6. Payment disputes

Please contact us first if a payment appears incorrect or a licence has not been delivered, so we can investigate promptly. This request does not prevent you from contacting your bank, Paystack or the relevant consumer-protection authority, or from exercising any legal right.
