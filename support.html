Common questions

Quick answers.

Is SA Invoice Pro only for IT repair shops?

It is particularly suited to IT repair shops, technicians and MSPs, but other service businesses may also find the invoicing and job-management workflows useful.

Does choosing a plan start recurring billing?

The current pricing flow is for a single licence purchase. Do not assume a recurring subscription unless a plan explicitly states otherwise.

What should I do if checkout returns to the site?

A return to the website does not alone confirm payment. Keep the PayFast reference and contact support if the licence is not available after the payment has been verified.

Where do I open the application?

Use the production app link at sa-invoice-pro.pcrepairdex.workers.dev.
