Before checkout

Know what to expect.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The app provides a 7-day trial with limited access to clients, invoices and quotes.

Are these monthly prices?

No. Solo, Shop and Multi-branch are annual software licence plans. The displayed amounts are yearly guide prices.

How do I pay?

Open the licence page in SA Invoice Pro and follow the Paystack checkout. Payment methods available to you are displayed by Paystack at checkout.

What happens after payment?

The billing service verifies the transaction before issuing a licence or activation key. Returning from checkout alone does not activate a licence.

Where can I read the refund terms?

Read our Refund & Cancellation Policy before purchasing.
