Before you buy

Questions, answered.

How does licence activation work?

Use the same email address at checkout and in SA Invoice Pro. After the billing service confirms the payment and issues the licence, open the app's License section to activate your account.

Does the price renew automatically?

The current checkout implementation is a single-payment flow, not an automatic recurring subscription. We will only describe or charge recurring billing after the subscription flow is configured and tested.

Can I pay if checkout says PayFast is not configured?

Not yet. The PayFast merchant ID and key must be configured by the product operator. Please do not send payment to an alternative account; contact sainvoicepro@gmail.com for assistance.

What if I use the wrong email?

Contact support from the email address you intended to use and include your payment reference. Never send your password or PayFast login details.
