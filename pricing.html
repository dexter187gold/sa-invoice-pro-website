Before checkout

Know what to expect.

How does payment work?

Select a plan and enter the email address for your licence. The billing service prepares the PayFast checkout. Never share your payment passphrase or account credentials.

Will I be charged every month?

No recurring billing is represented here. These displayed plans are single-purchase licence prices.

What happens after payment?

PayFast returns you to the website. The licence is issued only after the billing service verifies the payment notification.
